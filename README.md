# Muchiri Collins Kamama

Applied data science inside impact measurement. Monitoring, evaluation and data systems for UN agencies, NGOs, county governments and donor-funded programmes in Kenya and East Africa.

I build the layer underneath the framework: the ODK and KoboToolbox instruments, the PostgreSQL databases, the Python that cleans and analyses, the Power BI models that report, and the documentation that lets the system survive my departure.

## Portfolio artifacts

1. [mck-sdg-east-africa-analysis](https://github.com/collins-muchiri/mck-sdg-east-africa-analysis), applied statistical analysis of public UN SDG data for East Africa, including a demonstration that a regional mean moves seven percentage points under a different sampling rule with no observation changed.
2. [mck-rag-case-study](https://github.com/collins-muchiri/mck-rag-case-study), an engineering case study of a production retrieval-augmented generation system over UN-Habitat SDG 11 documentation, with decisions, trade-offs and numbers stated alongside their measurement status.
3. [mck-dashboard-to-insight](https://github.com/collins-muchiri/mck-dashboard-to-insight), a dashboard-to-insight write-up on a fully synthetic programme dashboard, showing how a coverage gap becomes a reporting decision.
